# Blog posts

Your articles appear on the **Blog** page (newest first), in the *Blog* section of the home page, in their category pages and in the sitemap.

## Writing a post

Go to **Posts → Add New**. The editor works like the portfolio editor (see [Portfolio projects](portfolio-projects.md)):

- **Title**: the post's headline.
- **Content**: type your article. Click **+** to add headings, images, lists or quotes between paragraphs. Use **Heading** blocks to break up long posts; they make articles easier to read and help search engines.
- **Featured image**: in the right-hand panel. It's shown on the post's card and at the top of the post. Landscape, at least 1200px wide.
- **Excerpt**: the short summary shown on the post's card (about 20 words). Without one, the card uses the first words of the post.
- **Categories**: in the right-hand panel, under **Categories**. Create new ones there or under **Posts → Categories**.
- **Publish**: makes the post live. To publish later, click the date next to **Publish** and pick a future date first.

## How many posts per page

The Blog page shows 9 posts at a time, with page numbers below. Change it under **Settings → Reading → Blog pages show at most**. Keep it to a multiple of 3 (6, 9, 12) so the last row of the grid is always full.

## Comments

Visitors can comment on posts. A person's **first** comment waits for your approval; after that, their comments appear straight away. Approve, reply to or delete comments under **Comments** in the left menu. Mark unwanted comments as **Spam** rather than deleting them; it helps WordPress recognise spam.
